Design takeaways

Headings now render as headings — the transcript is the primary reading surface, so hierarchy in assistant prose is not optional.

  • Content layer stays opaque; chrome floats above it as glass.
  • One material recipe, three size tiers, one motion vocabulary.
  • Every translucent surface carries a worst-case legibility plate.

The current Glass step composites 72% popover over anything — headings read straight through menus. The proposed plate slides with Material but is clamped to a worst-case AA floor, exactly the machinery Apple retrofitted in beta 3.

Two curves, three durations, all tokens — replacing 13 ad-hoc durations and 5 easing families found in the audit. Everything gated by prefers-reduced-motion and the app Motion axis (which desktop currently ignores).

Decision

Four directions

All four share the same Phase-1 foundation (bug fixes, token unification, motion system — none of it optional). They differ in how far the material goes. Click a card to preview it on the stage.

RECOMMENDED

A · Layered Chrome

Apple's two-tier discipline: content panes stay opaque; sidebar, bars, composer, popovers and palette float above as regular glass with tint floors and scroll-edge scrims.

  • Transcript scrolls under floating composer + topbar
  • Sidebar becomes chrome-layer glass over ambient tint
  • Popovers morph from their trigger, springy
  • Material axis finally means one thing everywhere
Risk: low–medium · Perf: bounded (≤4 glass regions)
AMBITIOUS

B · Full Vibrancy

The whole window is material — desktop shows through the shell (Electron vibrancy), content floats as opaque plates on glass. The most striking, the most costly.

  • Window-level translucency, wallpaper-lit chrome
  • Content cards keep full opacity for text
  • Requires Electron vibrancy + browser fallback story
  • Terminal/xterm needs an opaque-plate carve-out
Risk: high · Perf: window-wide blur, GPU-heavy
CONSERVATIVE

C · Quiet Glass

Today's layout, systematized: one glass recipe on transient surfaces only (menus, palette, toasts, dialogs). Fixes everything, changes little.

  • All 160 audit findings addressed
  • Chrome stays opaque slabs, borders soften
  • Fastest to ship, least visual payoff
Risk: minimal · Perf: unchanged
SHOWPIECE

D · Liquid

Direction A plus the garnish tier: Chromium-only edge refraction on hero surfaces, pointer-tracked bezels, elastic micro-physics on the composer.

  • SVG displacement (SDF maps) on popover + palette only
  • Pointer bezel + press illumination via CSS vars
  • Safari/reduced-motion tier is Direction A exactly
  • Ships behind a "Refraction" appearance setting
Risk: medium · Perf: budgeted, 2 refracting surfaces max
Recommendation: A now, D's garnish behind a setting once A lands. A is the direction a senior designer ships: it fixes the legibility failures the current Glass step has, gives the app a real material identity in both themes, keeps code/diff/terminal surfaces sacred, and its perf ceiling is known. B reads as a wallpaper demo in an IDE and puts the terminal at war with its own background; C leaves the product looking the way it does today. D is A with dessert — worth building, not worth blocking on.

Execution

Phased plan

PHASE 1

Foundation (direction-agnostic, starts immediately)

Define the material/elevation/motion token set (incl. sidebar family, added to THEME_TOKEN_NAMES); unify the 12+ ad-hoc recipes onto it; give the Material axis real blur tiers + tint floors; wire reduced-motion/transparency through desktop primitives; fix verified bugs (markdown headings, dead animate-in, combobox double-paint, scrim snap, WCO gaps, keyboard a11y).

PHASE 2

Direction build-out

Chrome layering per the chosen direction: floating composer + topbar with scroll-under transcript and scrims, glass sidebar, popover/palette morph motion, concentric radii, settings IA update (Material relabel, Motion axis on desktop, Refraction toggle placeholder).

PHASE 3

Garnish + hardening

Chromium-only refraction on popover/palette (SDF displacement, engine-gated, budgeted), pointer bezel, elastic press physics; perf pass (blur region audit, will-change discipline); browser-suite + typecheck + visual QA in both themes at every Material step.
